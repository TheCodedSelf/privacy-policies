Retro Keypad Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Your typing

Retro Keypad does not collect or send the text you type, surrounding text, or clipboard contents to the developer. The keyboard uses your device to enter text into the app you choose. That app handles text under its own privacy practices. Text in Retro Keypad’s optional demo is kept temporarily in memory and cleared when you leave or background the app.

Settings and purchases

Retro Keypad stores your selected keypad appearance, custom design, typing pause, and purchase status on your device so the app and keyboard can use your choices. Typing works offline. The app has no account or advertising.

Apple processes purchases and restores through the App Store. Retro Keypad uses Apple’s purchase status to enable the optional Retro Collection styles. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for information about Apple’s handling of purchase data.

App analytics and diagnostics

The containing app automatically sends app interactions, purchase and restore outcomes, operational logs, crash diagnostics, and a randomly generated installation identifier to a third-party analytics service hosted in the EU. We use this information to understand app usage, improve features, and troubleshoot problems. The identifier links activity from the same installation; it does not contain your name or email. The service also receives your IP address when the app connects. We do not use this data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

This collection does not include your writing, surrounding text, clipboard contents, or activity in the installed keyboard. There is no in-app analytics switch. Analytics and crash events have a one-year retention period; operational logs have a 14-day retention period. Expired data may be deleted later in batches. Removing the app does not delete data already sent.
