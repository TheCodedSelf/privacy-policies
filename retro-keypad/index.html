Retro Keypad Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Your typing

Retro Keypad does not collect or send the text you type, surrounding text, or clipboard contents to the developer. The keyboard uses your device to enter text into the app you choose. That app handles text under its own privacy practices. Text in Retro Keypad’s optional demo is kept temporarily in memory and cleared when you leave or background the app.

Settings and purchases

Retro Keypad stores your selected keypad appearance, custom design, typing pause, and purchase status on your device so the app and keyboard can use your choices. Typing works offline. The app has no account, advertising, tracking, analytics, or developer-operated server.

Apple processes purchases and restores through the App Store. Retro Keypad uses Apple’s purchase status to enable the optional Retro Collection styles. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for information about Apple’s handling of purchase data.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with the new date.
